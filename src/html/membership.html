Notary Member Program

The Toronto Official Documents Services office is the only designated authority in Ontario that issues apostille certificates. By partnering with us, you ensure that documents are professionally processed at this office, saving you and your clients the time and travel.

ApostilleCertificates.ca is building a province-wide network of independent notaries who wish to offer their clients seamless access to Ontario apostille services.

This program allows you to expand your service offering without handling the apostille process yourself. You continue to operate independently and retain full control of your notary practice and fees.

Member Benefits

Client Referrals from ApostilleCertificates.ca

We actively market apostille services across Ontario. As a member:

  • a. Your business name and address will be listed as an approved local notarization location.
  • b. Clients seeking apostilles in your region will be directed to you to complete their notarization.
  • c. Your office becomes a recognized local drop-off point prior to apostille processing.

This increases foot traffic and generates additional notary revenue without marketing expense on your end.

You Retain 100% of Your Notary Fees

Members:

  • a. Set and control their own notarization fees.
  • b. Collect and retain all notary fees directly from clients.
  • c. Operate independently with no interference in pricing structure.

ApostilleCertificates.ca charges only for the apostille processing portion.

Streamlined Apostille Processing

After notarization:

  • a. The client (or your office) forwards the documents to ApostilleCertificates.ca.
  • b. We attend at Official Documents Services for apostille issuance.
  • c. Certified documents are returned by registered mail either:
  • 1. Directly to the client; or
  • 2. Back to your office, as directed.
  • d. ApostilleCertificates.ca services commence upon receipt of the documents and once payment is completed whether payment is made directly by the client or coordinated through the notary member.

You do not need to attend at an apostille office or manage the administrative process.

Expanded Service Offering Without Operational Burden

By participating:

  • a. You can confidently offer “Apostille Services Available” to your clients.
  • b. You avoid time spent travelling to apostille offices.
  • c. You avoid dealing with processing queues and administrative requirements.
  • d. You increase perceived value of your practice.

You remain the client’s local professional contact while we handle the backend apostille logistics.

Increased Online Visibility

As part of the membership:

  • a. Your address will be published on ApostilleCertificates.ca as an approved notarization partner.
  • b. You agree to place an ApostilleCertificates.ca backlink logo on your website.

This cross-linking strengthens online authority and improves search visibility for both parties.

Member Requirements

To participate, members agree to:

  1. Permit ApostilleCertificates.ca to publish their business name and address on its website.
  2. Allow ApostilleCertificates.ca to use their address on social media, online maps (e.g.,Google Maps, Apple Maps), and similar platforms, designating their office as an official apostille drop-off location.
  3. Display a provided ApostilleCertificates.ca backlink logo on their website.
  4. Provide professional notarization services to clients prior to apostille submission.
  5. Forward documents promptly when acting as a drop-off point.

Members remain fully independent and are not employees, agents, or representatives of ApostilleCertificates.ca.

Document Flow Summary

  1. Client contacts ApostilleCertificates.ca.
  2. Client is directed to a local member notary for notarization.
  3. Notary completes notarization and collects their fee.
  4. Documents are sent to ApostilleCertificates.ca, either by client directly or by notary.
  5. Apostille is obtained.
  6. Documents are returned to the client or notary via registered mail as directed.

Term and Termination

Membership operates on an ongoing basis.

Either party may terminate membership with thirty (30) days’ written notice.

Upon termination, listings and backlink requirements will be removed.
