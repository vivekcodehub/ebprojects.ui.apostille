Notary Member Program

The Toronto Official Documents Services office is the only designated authority in Ontario that issues apostille certificates. By partnering with us, you ensure that documents are professionally processed at this office, saving you and your clients the time and travel.

ApostilleCertificates.ca is building a province-wide network of independent notaries who wish to offer their clients seamless access to Ontario apostille services.

This program allows you to expand your service offering without handling the apostille process yourself. You continue to operate independently and retain full control of your notary practice and fees.

Join the Network

If you are a licensed Ontario notary public and wish to expand your services to include apostille access for your clients, we welcome your participation. Contact us to request membership details and onboarding information.
